Looks for ONVIF-compatible cameras on this network. Most modern IP/WiFi cameras support it. Found a camera? Type its username & password (the ones from the camera's app/label) and click Add.

Paste a camera stream link. Works with rtsp://, onvif://, or http:// (MJPEG) links.

Don't know your camera's link? Two ways to get it:

  1. Try 🔎 Scan WiFi first — it finds most cameras automatically.
  2. Otherwise, find the camera's IP (in your router or the camera's phone app) and use the pattern for your brand below. Replace USER, PASS, and the IP.
BrandTypical RTSP link (main stream)
Reolinkrtsp://USER:PASS@IP:554/h264Preview_01_main
Hikvision / Annkertsp://USER:PASS@IP:554/Streaming/Channels/101
Dahua / Amcrestrtsp://USER:PASS@IP:554/cam/realmonitor?channel=1&subtype=0
TP-Link Taportsp://USER:PASS@IP:554/stream1
Ubiquiti UniFirtsp://IP:7447/<stream-id> (enable RTSP per camera)
Wyze (with RTSP firmware)rtsp://USER:PASS@IP/live
Any ONVIF cameraonvif://USER:PASS@IP:80 (go2rtc finds the RTSP link for you)

Tip: cameras usually have a low-res “sub” stream too (lighter on WiFi). For Hikvision that's .../Channels/102; Dahua uses subtype=1.

Cloud-only cameras (stock Ring, Nest, Wyze without RTSP firmware) block local access, so they usually can't be monitored this way.
